 THE HUMAN FIREWALL
 Personal Cybersecurity Checklist

Use this checklist to review your online safety habits.

 Account Security
- [ ] I use strong and unique passwords.
- [ ] I enable two-factor authentication.
- [ ] I never share my passwords or PINs.
- [ ] I log out of accounts on shared devices.

 Email and Messages
- [ ] I check the sender before opening messages.
- [ ] I avoid suspicious links.
- [ ] I do not open unexpected attachments.
- [ ] I verify unusual requests before responding.

 Device Security
- [ ] I update my software regularly.
- [ ] I use trusted security software.
- [ ] I lock my devices when not in use.
- [ ] I avoid downloading files from unknown sources.

 Reporting Incidents
- [ ] I report suspicious messages.
- [ ] I notify IT support about possible account compromise.
- [ ] I take action if I notice unusual account activity.

 Final Reminder
PAUSE. VERIFY. PROTECT.

Your Mind Is Your First Line of Defence.
