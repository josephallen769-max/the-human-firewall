THE HUMAN FIREWALL
Phishing Awareness Guide

1. Introduction
Phishing is a cybercrime in which attackers use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

2. Common Types of Phishing
- Email phishing: Fake emails designed to steal information.
- SMS phishing: Fraudulent text messages containing suspicious links.
- Spear phishing: Targeted messages aimed at specific individuals.
- Website phishing: Fake websites designed to look legitimate.

3. Warning Signs
- Unknown or suspicious senders.
- Messages requesting passwords or banking details.
- Unexpected attachments or links.
- Messages creating urgency or fear.
- Unusual website addresses.

4. How to Stay Protected
- Verify the sender before responding.
- Avoid clicking suspicious links.
- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Keep devices and software updated.
- Report suspicious messages.
 5. What to Do If You Suspect Phishing
1. Do not click the link or open the attachment.
2. Do not share personal information.
3. Report the message to the relevant IT support team.
4. Change affected passwords using the official website.
5. Monitor accounts for unusual activity.
